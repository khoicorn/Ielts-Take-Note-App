My Sentence

The number of visitors of the City Zoo increased steadily.

Band 7+ Upgrade

The number of visitors to the City Zoo increased steadily.

Why it is better

Use “visitors to + place”, not “visitors of + place”. The visitors travel to the zoo; they do not belong to it.

Example

The number of visitors to the City Zoo increased steadily from 35,000 to 68,000.

Reusable pattern

The number of visitors to increased steadily from to .
